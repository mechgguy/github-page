Towards Trustworthy and Explainable AI for Perception Models: From Concept to Prototype Vehicle Deployment

Conference Paper | IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC 2026)

Investigated the structural resilience, interpretability, and adversarial robustness of multimodal attention mechanisms integrating LiDAR point clouds and camera data. Developed a complete pipeline deployed on a prototype vehicle to evaluate out-of-distribution edge cases and sensor degradation.

T. Beemelmanns, S. Sharifi, M. Mehrotra, A. Choudhuri, L. Eckstein (2026). Towards Trustworthy and Explainable AI for Perception Models: From Concept to Prototype Vehicle Deployment. In IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC), Naples, Italy. [arXiv:2605.16087].
