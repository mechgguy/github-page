Integrating Traffic Light Status for MPC Planner in ACDC Suite

Developed the existing Model Predictive Control Planner incorporated by Automated and Connected Driving Challenges ACDC Research Project with C++, ROS to integrate traffic light status into the MPC planner. This involved modifying the planner to account for traffic light states, its position and its future states using SPAT and MAP messages from the traffic light recognition module.
